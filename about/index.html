About these guides

I'm Manikant Yadav, a developer from New Delhi, India, and the maker of SmallPDF.us. I write these guides because the same questions come up again and again: why is my PDF so big, why can't I open this photo, why does my converted document look wrong.

The guides are my own and are honest about limits. Where a tool cannot do something well, I say so. They are not independent reviews: SmallPDF.us is my own project and the guides link to it.
