How to open and convert HEIC photos from an iPhone

By Manikant Yadav, maker of SmallPDF.us · Updated 2026-10-13

You send an iPhone photo to a friend or try to upload it to a website, and it fails. The reason is almost always the format: since iOS 11, iPhones save photos as HEIC by default.

What is HEIC?

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Format) stores a photo in about half the space of a JPG at similar quality. That is why Apple uses it. The downside is compatibility: many Windows PCs, older Android phones, and plenty of websites and printers cannot read it without extra software.

Option 1: convert after the fact

  1. Open HEIC to JPG.
  2. Select your photos. You can convert several at once.
  3. Download the JPG files.

Need transparency or no quality loss? Use HEIC to PNG instead, knowing the files will be larger.

Option 2: change the iPhone setting

Go to Settings, Camera, Formats and choose Most Compatible. New photos are then saved as JPG. Existing photos stay as they are.

Option 3: let iOS convert when sharing

When you share a photo with AirDrop or Mail, iOS can convert it for you in some cases. Behaviour depends on the app and the iOS version, so do not rely on it for important uploads.

After converting

  • Photos from a modern iPhone are several MB each. Reduce them with Compress Image before you email or upload them.
  • Want them in one document? Put the JPGs into a PDF with JPG to PDF.
Orientation: a correctly converted photo keeps its original rotation. If a result looks sideways, check the original on your phone first.
