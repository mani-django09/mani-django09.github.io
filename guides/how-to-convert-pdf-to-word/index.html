How to convert a PDF to an editable Word document

By Manikant Yadav, maker of SmallPDF.us · Updated 2026-10-13

PDF is made for reading and printing, not editing. Converting to Word gets you an editable copy, but how good the result is depends on what kind of PDF you start with.

Step 1: find out what kind of PDF you have

Open the PDF and try to select a sentence with your mouse.

  • You can select text: it is a "digital" PDF. Conversion usually works very well.
  • You cannot select anything: it is a scan, which is just pictures of pages. You need OCR (text recognition) first, using OCR PDF, which adds a text layer. Then convert.

Step 2: convert

  1. Open PDF to Word and upload the file.
  2. Wait for the conversion and download the .docx.
  3. Open it in Word, LibreOffice or Google Docs.

What usually needs a quick fix

ProblemWhy it happensFix
Lines break in odd placesPDF stores every line separatelySelect the paragraph and remove manual line breaks
Tables shift slightlyPDF has no real table structureAdjust column widths, or retype small tables
Wrong fontThe font is not installed on your computerInstall the font or choose a similar one
Missing charactersUnusual symbols or fonts without text dataCompare with the PDF and retype them

Going the other way

When you have finished editing, turn it back into a PDF with Word to PDF. For small changes to an existing PDF (a date, a name), Edit PDF is often faster than converting at all.

Be realistic: no converter reproduces every PDF perfectly. Brochures with many columns, overlapping images or unusual fonts will always need some cleanup.
